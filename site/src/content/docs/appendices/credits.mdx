## LearnOpenGL

This series follows the chapter structure, topic ordering and teaching approach of
**[LearnOpenGL](https://learnopengl.com)** by **Joey de Vries**
([@JoeyDeVriez](https://twitter.com/JoeyDeVriez)).

LearnOpenGL is the best introduction to real-time graphics that exists, and the reason
it works is the order it puts things in: the decision to treat lighting and physically
based rendering as essential rather than advanced, the insistence that each chapter
continues the last, the refusal to stop at the triangle. That shape is what has been
borrowed here.

The prose and the code are new. Vulkan differs enough from OpenGL that adapting
explanations sentence by sentence would produce worse ones than writing them fresh, and
every line of C++ and Slang in this repository was written for it.

Where a figure from LearnOpenGL appears, it is reproduced under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) with attribution to Joey de
Vries in the caption, as that licence requires.
